What You Get
Everything a voxel world needs

Built from scratch with WebGL. No Unity, no Unreal — pure browser performance.

🌍

Infinite Procedural Terrain

Multi-octave noise generation creates unique landscapes every session — plains, hills, mountains, and underground cave systems.

⛏️

Mine & Place Blocks

Left-click to mine any block in the world. Right-click to place from your hotbar. Real raycasting engine with face detection.

🔮

16 Block Types

Grass, dirt, stone, sand, wood, leaves, brick, glass, coal ore, iron ore, gold ore, diamond, TNT, obsidian, snow, and water.

⚡

Real Physics Engine

Full collision detection, gravity, jumping, and sprinting. Toggle fly mode for creative building without limits.

🌲

Biomes & Trees

Procedurally generated forests, snow biomes, and desert regions. Trees grow naturally across the landscape.

🖥️

Zero Install

Runs entirely in your browser via WebGL. Works on Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari. No account required for free play.
